Frequently asked questions about Massachusetts home prices

Which county in Massachusetts has the most expensive homes?

Nantucket County has the highest median home value in Massachusetts at $3,158,782. This is often searched as the "richest" or "wealthiest" county in Massachusetts — though it's worth being precise about what's being measured here: median home value reflects property prices, not household income or net worth. The two usually correlate, but a county can have expensive homes without having the highest-earning residents, particularly in areas with a lot of vacation or second homes.

Which county in Massachusetts has the highest household income?

Nantucket County has the highest median household income in Massachusetts at $139,688. If what you're really after is the "richest" or "wealthiest" county in Massachusetts, this is the closer answer — income measures what households actually earn each year, while home values measure what property costs. Neither is quite the same as wealth, which would also account for savings, investments and property people already own outright.

What is the cheapest county in Massachusetts?

Hampden County has the lowest median home value of the 14 Massachusetts counties tracked here, at $357,811. Keep in mind that low home prices often reflect limited local job markets, distance from major metro areas, or shrinking population — affordability alone doesn't tell you whether somewhere is a good fit.

How do Massachusetts home prices compare to the rest of the country?

The typical (median) county in Massachusetts has a home value of $694,439, against $234,352 for the median U.S. county — roughly 196.3% higher. Figures come from Zillow's Home Value Index and are refreshed daily on this site.
