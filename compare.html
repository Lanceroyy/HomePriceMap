    What these figures mean

    Home value is Zillow's Home Value Index for the broad middle of the local housing market, not a simple average sale price. Price to income divides that value by Census median household income. FBI city crime figures are police-department reports; county figures combine the reporting cities that could be matched inside that county and may not cover every resident.
